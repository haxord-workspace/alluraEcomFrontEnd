# 📸 Allura Boutique — Image Upload Guide

You can easily drop your own high-resolution boutique photographs into these organized folders.
Whenever you drop an image with the suggested filenames below, you can link them directly in the app!

---

## 📂 Folder Structure

```
public/
└── images/
    ├── hero-banners/          -> Main homepage top carousel banners
    ├── shop-by-occasion/      -> The 5 arched occasion category cards
    ├── best-sellers/          -> Product photos (primary, back view, gallery)
    ├── editorial-banners/     -> "New Arrivals" & "The Allura Collection" promo cards
    ├── boutique-experience/   -> "The Allura Experience" video & boutique showcase
    ├── instagram/             -> "As Seen on Instagram" gallery tiles (7 photos)
    ├── lookbook/              -> Seasonal lookbook editorial photos
    └── about/                 -> Storefront, founder story & heritage photos
```

---

## 🏷️ Recommended File Names & Dimensions

### 1. `hero-banners/` (Homepage Top Slider)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9` or `21:9` wide landscape (e.g. `1920x1080px` or `1800x900px`)
- **Suggested Filenames**:
  - `slide-1.jpg` (Designer Ethnic / Style for Every Occasion)
  - `slide-2.jpg` (Royal Bridal Couture)
  - `slide-3.jpg` (Contemporary Modern Silhouettes)

---

### 2. `shop-by-occasion/` (5 Arched Cards)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `4:5` or `3:4` vertical portrait (e.g. `800x1000px`)
- **Suggested Filenames**:
  - `bridal.jpg` (Bridal Edit)
  - `festive.jpg` (Festive Celebrations)
  - `party-wear.jpg` (Party Wear)
  - `western.jpg` (Everyday Western Elegance)
  - `ethnic.jpg` (Traditional & Ethnic Wear)

---

### 3. `best-sellers/` (Product Garment Photography)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `4:5` vertical portrait (e.g. `1000x1250px`)
- **Suggested Filenames**:
  - `anarkali-primary.jpg`, `anarkali-secondary.jpg`
  - `coord-primary.jpg`, `coord-secondary.jpg`
  - `kurta-primary.jpg`, `kurta-secondary.jpg`
  - `maxi-primary.jpg`, `maxi-secondary.jpg`
  - `bridal-lehenga-primary.jpg`, `bridal-lehenga-secondary.jpg`
  - `emerald-silk-primary.jpg`, `emerald-silk-secondary.jpg`

---

### 4. `editorial-banners/` (Middle Promo Banners)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `16:10` or `16:9` landscape (e.g. `1200x750px`)
- **Suggested Filenames**:
  - `new-arrivals.jpg`
  - `allura-collection.jpg`

---

### 5. `boutique-experience/` (Store & Story Video Card)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `4:5` vertical portrait (e.g. `1000x1250px`)
- **Suggested Filename**:
  - `boutique-store.jpg`

---

### 6. `instagram/` (Social Feed Grid)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `1:1` square (e.g. `600x600px` or `800x800px`)
- **Suggested Filenames**:
  - `insta-1.jpg` to `insta-7.jpg`

---

### 7. `lookbook/` & `about/`
- `lookbook/`: `look-1.jpg`, `look-2.jpg`, `look-3.jpg`, `look-4.jpg`
- `about/`: `storefront.jpg`, `craftsmanship.jpg`
